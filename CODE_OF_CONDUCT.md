# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Showing empathy and kindness toward other people
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Accepting responsibility, apologising to those affected by our mistakes, and
  learning from the experience
- Focusing on what is best for the project, not just for us individually

Unacceptable behaviour:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address,
  without their explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Technical disagreement is not misconduct

This project argues about engineering in writing, sometimes bluntly: a review
that says a claim is false, or that a design is wrong, is doing its job. The line
is the difference between *"this measurement does not support that claim"* and a
remark about the person who made it. Criticism of work is welcome and expected.
Contempt for people is not.

## Scope

This Code applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions and
commit messages — and when an individual is officially representing the project
in public.

## Enforcement

Report abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behaviour to
**conduct@andyur.ai**. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly
and fairly, and the reporter's privacy and security will be respected.

Andyur has one maintainer, so enforcement is that person's decision. Where they
have a conflict of interest they will say so and recuse themselves from the
decision rather than decide anyway.

Consequences escalate with severity and repetition: a private warning, a
temporary ban from interaction, or a permanent ban.
